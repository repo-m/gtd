# 2025-04-20 Electromagnetic Spectrum

| #  | Name                    | Frequency Range         | 10^x Notation                   | Typical Usage                           |
|----|-------------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| 1  | ELF (Extremely Low)     | 3 Hz – 300 Hz            | 10⁰ – 10² Hz                     | Submarine communication, geology        |
| 2  | AF (Audio Frequency)    | 20 Hz – 20 kHz           | 2×10¹ – 2×10⁴ Hz                 | Human hearing, music, audio systems     |
| 3  | VLF (Very Low)          | 300 Hz – 3 kHz           | 10² – 10³ Hz                     | Navigation, submarine comms             |
| 4  | LF (Low)                | 3 kHz – 30 kHz           | 10³ – 10⁴ Hz                     | Navigation systems (LORAN), AM longwave |
| 5  | MF (Medium)             | 30 kHz – 300 kHz         | 10⁴ – 10⁵ Hz                     | AM radio                                |
| 6  | HF (High)               | 300 kHz – 3 MHz          | 10⁵ – 10⁶ Hz                     | Shortwave radio, amateur radio          |
| 7  | VHF (Very High)         | 3 MHz – 30 MHz           | 10⁶ – 10⁷ Hz                     | FM radio, TV, air traffic control       |
| 8  | UHF (Ultra High)        | 30 MHz – 300 MHz         | 10⁷ – 10⁸ Hz                     | TV, mobile phones, GPS                  |
| 9  | SHF (Super High)        | 300 MHz – 3 GHz          | 10⁸ – 10⁹ Hz                     | Wi-Fi, radar, satellite comms           |
| 10 | EHF (Extremely High)    | 3 GHz – 30 GHz           | 10⁹ – 10¹⁰ Hz                    | Advanced radar, 5G, microwave links     |
| 11 | THz (Terahertz)         | 30 GHz – 3 THz           | 10¹⁰ – 10¹² Hz                   | Security scans, spectroscopy            |
| 12 | Infrared                | 3 THz – 430 THz          | 10¹² – 4.3×10¹⁴ Hz               | Remote controls, thermal imaging        |
| 13 | Visible Light           | 430 THz – 750 THz        | 4.3×10¹⁴ – 7.5×10¹⁴ Hz           | Human vision, photography               |
| 14 | Ultraviolet (UV)        | 750 THz – 30 PHz         | 7.5×10¹⁴ – 3×10¹⁶ Hz             | Sterilization, black lights             |
| 15 | X-Rays                  | 30 PHz – 30 EHz          | 3×10¹⁶ – 3×10¹⁹ Hz               | Medical imaging, airport security       |
| 16 | Gamma Rays              | >30 EHz                  | >3×10¹⁹ Hz                       | Nuclear physics, cancer treatment       |
