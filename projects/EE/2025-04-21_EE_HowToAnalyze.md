# 2025-04-21 EE | How To Analyze & Tools

1. **Function**  
   - What does the device do? (e.g., light up, move, measure, communicate)
2. **Energy Flow**  
   - Where does the power come from?  
   - How is it converted, regulated, and consumed?
3. **Signal & Control**  
   - How does it process or respond to data?  
   - Is there a microcontroller, logic circuit, or feedback mechanism?
4. **Key Components**  
   - Identify major parts:  
     - Power supply  
     - Sensors/actuators  
     - Microcontrollers or ICs  
     - Displays or outputs
5. **Schematic / Circuit Analysis**  
   - Trace the **power path** (input → regulation → output)  
   - Follow **signal paths** (sensor → processor → actuator)  
   - Identify standard **circuit blocks** (e.g., op-amps, transistors)  
   - Use **labels and values** to interpret behavior

## Devices

### 🛠️ Basic Tools

- **Multimeter** – Measure voltage, current, resistance
- **Oscilloscope** – Visualize voltage signals over time
- **Power Supply Unit (PSU)** – Provide controlled voltage/current
- **Soldering Iron** – Modify or repair circuits
- **Breadboard & jumper wires** – Build and test simple circuits

### 🧠 Diagnostic Tools

- **Logic Analyzer** – Analyze digital signals and protocols
- **LCR Meter** – Measure inductance, capacitance, resistance precisely
- **Function Generator** – Create test waveforms (sine, square, etc.)

### 🖥️ Software Tools

- **Circuit simulation software** (e.g., LTspice, Multisim)
- **PCB viewer/editor** (e.g., KiCad, Eagle)
- **Datasheet access** – For interpreting component specs

### 🔎 Optional Advanced Tools

- **Thermal Camera or IR thermometer** – Detect overheating components
- **Signal Analyzer / Spectrum Analyzer** – For RF or audio signals
