# STAGE S5.1: The Preparation

- Start preparation 4 weeks before presentation/speech
- What is my message?
- What is my motivation and/or passion?
- What do I want to transport via my presentation/speech?
- What should my audience remember out of my presentation/speech?
- Who is in my audience?
- What do they know?
- Do a research about topic (studies, actual news, personal relation, story telling, provocing argument, ciritic, )
  - You should know 40 times more than your audience
- Information about location
  - How is the room?
  - What about the technic? Avoid technical problems
  - Where will the audience be?
- Plan outfit
