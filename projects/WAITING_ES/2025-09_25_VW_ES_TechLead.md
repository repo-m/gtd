# Tech lead System Integration & Cross-functional (f/m/d) TB 802/F

## Kurzbeschreibung der Rolle

We are rethinking how software is developed at V. In partnership with our joint venture, R and V Group Technologies, LLC we aim to bring next-level software developed practices to VTE. As part of this transformation, we are enabling part of TE with new competencies and are looking for proactive contributors with hands-on experience.

As System Integration and Cross-Functional Lead, you will be responsible for end-to-end integration of complex automotive software systems, enduring seamless system behavior across software and hardware domains as well as vehicle functions across all development phases.

This role includes a 6-month short-term assignment in Palo Alto (California, USA), working directly on-site in a dynamic software development environment. During this time you will actively contribute to tech stack, return with documented learnings and serve as an expert in your team.

It's unique & exciting opportunity for driven individuals who want to make an impact and grow in a fast-paces tech setting.

Are you ready to shape the future of software-defined mobility with us?

## Mögliche Aufgaben dieser Rolle

1. Leading and mentoring the System Integration & Cross-functional Team
2. Coordinating activities, including HiL & prototype readiness, to align with integration milestones
3. Supporting software merges & platform transitions with structured build validation & functional testing
4. Handling interface agreements and change tracking across disciplines and lifecycle phases
5. Developing & executing test plans for integrated systems across HiL, vehicle mule and fleet

## Anforderungen an die Qualifikation

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Mathematics or Computer Science
- 8+ years of experience in embedded systems integration, automotive system test or manuf. Integration
- Minimum B2 level English proficiency required
- Experience in leading multidisciplinary teams
- Open to relocation to the U.S. for onboarding and training (6-9 months)
