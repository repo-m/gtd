# Electrical Power Supply System / EBN - Energiebordnetz - #VDA450

- Process
    - #FuSa #ISO26262
    - Fail-Active (Machine is fallback)
- Design
    - Redundancy: 2x Sources + eFuses
    - Monitoring: Source (SOH), Voltage (Normal, Upper/Lower, Critical)
    - Integrity: Power Management
- Test
    - Load Shedding
    - Validate: no SPF (single point of failure) causes total power loss.
- Overhead: Costs, Complexity, Active Power Management
