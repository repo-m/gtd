- Book: Geoffrey Greif - Buddy System: Understanding Male Friendships
- **Must friend**: a best friend, a member of your inner circle, a person you count on when something big happens in your life
- **Trust friend**: a friend who shows integrity, someone you feel comfortable with, that you're always glad to see, but not in your inmost circle; perhaps someone you'd like to be closer to, if you had the time or opportunity
- **Rust friend**: a person you've known for a long, long time; you're probably not going to get any closer to that person, unless something changes, but a part of your life
- **Just friends**: a person you see -- at a weekly poker game, at your child's school -- who is enjoyable company, but you have no desire to socialize outside a specific context or to get to know that person better

Friends!

We all have different types of friends. You see, friendships are like a box of chocolates – some are sweet, some are nutty, and some are just plain weird. But each one has its own special place in our hearts. So, let's dive into the categories of friendship!

First up, we have the **Must Friend**. This is your best friend, your ride-or-die, the person who knows all your secrets and still likes you anyway. For me, that's my brother Abdulkerim. We've been through thick and thin together. Remember that time I washed his hair after he set off a firework in our kitchen and burned his hair. Yeah, that's the kind of bond we share. A Must Friend is the one you call when you win the lottery or when you need bail money – sometimes both on the same day!

Next, we have the **Trust Friend**. This is someone you always enjoy seeing, someone with great vibes. They're not in your inner circle, but you wouldn't mind if they were. For me, that's Cengiz. We see each other very seldom, but when we do, it’s always special. Trust Friends are like Wi-Fi – you don't always see them, but you know they're there, and they make your life better.

Then there's the **Rust Friend**. This is someone you've known forever. You've grown up together, and while you may not be as close as you once were, they're still a part of your life. For me, that's Kai. We've known each other since primary school. Rust Friends are like that old pair of jeans you can't throw away – comfortable, reliable, and full of memories, even if they don't fit quite like they used to.

And finally, we have the **Just Friends**. These are the people you see in specific contexts – at your child's school, or maybe at the gym. They're enjoyable company, but you have no desire to take the relationship further. For me, that's Emrah. We share some laughs, maybe a few inside jokes, but that's where it ends. Just Friends are like the toppings on a pizza – they add flavor, but they're not the main ingredient.

So, here's they are - the Must Friends, the Trust Friends, the Rust Friends, and the Just Friends. Each one brings something unique to the table, and together, they make our lives richer, funnier, and lot more interesting!
