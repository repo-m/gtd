# #Confucius (551-479 BCE, Oufu)

## Thesis

- He was from a will to do family
- faithfulness and sincerity are shown in traditional rituals and ceremonies. These qualities in these settings allow virtue to become visible. Virtue is then made manifest in the world. Virtue can then be seen by others. Others are transformed by virtue. Faithfulness and sincerity hold the power of transformation.
