# GTD David #Allen

- "... the task of learning something new becomes a stress-free experience of joy and calmness..."
- "Anxiety is cause by a lack of control, organization, preparation, and action." David #Kekich
- Your ability to generate power is directly proportional to your ability to relax
- Transfer stuff into a clear inventory of meaningful actions, projects and useable information
- There is no reason to ever have the same thought twice, unless you like having that thought
- Any "would, could, or should" commitments held only in the psyche creates irrational and unresolvable pressure
- And if, while you have been reading this, you've been distracted by your mind wandering to other things going on in your life, or you've felt impelled to check e-mail for potentially meaningful new input, you're experiencing a manifestation of this **don't-miss-the-train syndrome**.
- We (1) **capture** what has our attention; (2) **clarify** what each item means and what to do about it; (3) **organize** the results, which presents the options we (4) **reflect** on, which we then choose to (5) engage with.
- Six Level Model
  - Horizon 5: Purpose & Principles
  - Horizon 4: Vision
  - Horizon 3: Goals
  - Horizon 2: Areas of focus and accountabilities
  - Horizon 1: Current projects
  - Ground: Current actions

## Lists

- **Stuff**
- **Project** duedate within  1 year + multistep actions
- **Project plans**
- **Next Action**
  - incl. Planning projects (see Planning Projects)
- **Calendar** exact hard edges of commitments !
  - time-specific actions: appointments
  - day-specific actions
  - day-specific information
    - incl. important reminders (start or due dates)
- **WaitingFor**
- **Backlog** Someday/maybe
  - incl. tickler list
- **Reference**
  - incl. project support material -> Use prefix
- **Checklists**
  - Training, diet, values, travel, weekly review, jokes

## Daily

- Check Calendar
- Check Next Actions
- daily blocker: Gather and process stuff

## Weekly

- Get clear: Gather and process all your stuff
- Get current
  - Review & update **Next Actions** list
  - Review & update previous **Calendar** data
  - Review & update upcoming **Calendar**
  - Review & update upcoming **WaitingFor** list
  - Review & update **Projects** list
  - Review & update **Checklists**
- Get creative
  - Review **Backlog**
  - *Are there any new, wonderful, harebrained, creative, thought-provoking, risk-taking, ideas?*

## Planning Projects

- WHY? Defining purpose and principles
- WHAT? Outcome visioning
  - Think of what a successful outcome would look like: where would you be physically, financially, in terms of reputation, or whatever?
  - How do you want the client to feel, and what do you want him to know and do after the presentation?
- HOW? Brainstorming
  - Brainstorm potential steps.
- Organizing - **Project plan**
  - Organize your ideas
- Identifying next actions -> **Next Action**
  - Decide on the next actions
