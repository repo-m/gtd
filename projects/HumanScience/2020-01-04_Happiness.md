# 2020-01-04_Happiness

According to Aristo there are 3 kinds of happiness one can reach. I can't remember how he named them exactly but I will call them

1. Animalistic happiness
2. Political happiness
3. Philosophical happiness

The first one is the category of happiness which we can easily observe on animals. So when is a monkey happy? When it has enough food, somewhere secure and warm enough to stay, friends, sex, progeny and so on. I would claim that most people are in this category. These people feel happy when they have money, property, children etc.
The next level of happiness is the one you can see on people who are driven from an idea like communism or a relegion. These people don't need big cars or much too eat. They don't care if they have a warm place to stay. For them there's nothing bigger then the idea which is for them the sense of life. So they live for their principles and rather die than betray those.
The highest level of happiness is according Aristo the philosophical Happiness. That's not suprising - he was a philosopher. People who taste this.kind of happiness aren't interested of a big house or to convince people of their ideas or try to get political and move people. This people are just interested in wisdom. It's like getting high from different perspectives, books, arguments, thinking etc.
