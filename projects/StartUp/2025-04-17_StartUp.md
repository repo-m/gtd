## 7 recipes for generating startup ideas

1. Start with what your team is especially good at
2. Start with a problem you personally have encountered
    1. Think of every job you've had (+internships + life experiences)
    2. What are problems or opportunities you've been in a special position to see?
3. Think of things you personally wish existed => danger of terpit ideas, always ask is there a reason this doesn't exist yet
4. Look for things in the world that have changed recently
5. look for new variants of successful companies
6. Talk to people and ask what problems they have => Analytic approach with lot of work
    1. Pick an idea space -> idea space which is not disrupted much like truck or old customers
    2. Talk to potential customer
    3. Ask founders in the idea space
    4. Evaluate ideas
7. Look for big industries that seem broken
8. Find a co-founder who already has an idea

## 10 Questions to challenge startup idea

1. Founder/Market fit? Pick the best idea for your team!
2. How big is the market? Big or rapetly growing market!
3. How acute is the problem?
4. Do you have competition?
5. Do you want this?
6. Did this recently become possible or necessary?
7. Are there good proxies for this business?
8. Is that an idea you want to work on for years?
9. Is this a scalable business?
10. Is this a good idea space?

## Y Combinator Video - Michael Seibel

- The initial idea does not need to be great -> not very original initial idea can later become a great idea
- Start with a Problem
  - keep problem notebook instead an idea notebook
- Personal connection to the problem is helpful
  - You can tell weather the solution is in the ballpark of solving the problem
  - When you're feeling discouraged, you still have a personal connection to see you through until you find a solution that does work
- Brainstorm with Friends
  - plan focused brainstorm sessions
  - friends, which have potential to be your co-founder
- Once You've Found an Interesting Problem
  - Why Are You Uniquely Qualified to Work on This Problem?
    - is there something on the problem you understand better than others?
    - look at other products or competitors who have tried to solve the same problem
  - two questions
    - why now?
    - whats different?
- Your MVP
  - get things in people hands
  - do not fall in love with it -> release in quickly!
  - it will solve the problem poorly
  - be in love with your problem
  - be in love with the customer => focus on customer do not focus on competetors
  - treat your product in a way that it can change
- Your Initial Users
  - Be carful who your initial users are
  - You need to pick them: the count of costumers does not matter. It is important that the customer has the problem and do your product solve the problem.
    - not people who also want to be founder!
- Growth matters for funding!!
  - Ads, Reference customers, Using = Sharing
  - usage is most important -> best ad!
