# #Zizek – Fantasy Over Reality

- #Zizek ’s “travel example”: he visits major cities but sometimes stays inside the hotel, consuming the city as an image rather than experiencing it directly.
- Core point: fantasy structures enjoyment — the idea of a place or experience is often more pleasurable than the messy real version.
- Parallel examples:
  - Enjoying the movie or imagined experience more than the real event.
  - Liking the neighbor or “other” only at a distance, as an abstract figure.
  - Decaffeinated culture: coffee without caffeine, beer without alcohol, virtual sex — reality stripped of risk.

**Insight: We often choose sanitized fantasies over direct encounters with reality.**

## Reference

- The Plague of Fantasies by #Zizek — explores how fantasy animates ideology and enjoyment.
- The Parallax View by #Zizek — covers his philosophical approach including reality, subjectivity and ideology.
