## 2023

### 2023-09-30 London Trip

#### Lessons learned London trip in 2023

- Check distance between hotel and airport -> organize transport
- flights: check costs for luggage and seats
- flights: times should be arrival hotel 2pm; departure at 10am from hotel
- pack 10x Brötchen
- pack small water Packs
- check how to get internet -> offline Google Maps is enough
- check transfer between airport and hotel
- never more than 3 days
- Sneaker!!!
- organize eatables -> check super market
- only eat specialities -> pick 2 restaurants
- check transportation within city
- never exchange money -> use credit card -> if needed 50€
- pack two credit cards -> needed for transportation bus
- photos: 2x full partner pictures each day!

#### Highlights from our London trip in 2023

- British Museum
- Shakespeare Globe theatre
- Musical Sunset BLVRD
- National Gallery 50%
- Starbucks
- Waterstones -> Book stores
