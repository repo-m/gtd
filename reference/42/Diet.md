# Diet Plan

- Do not watch anything during eating!
- **Nutrition & Structure**
 - Daily calories: 1660 kcal
 - Daily protein: 158 g
 - Cook own meals - daily 5pm
 - Zero sugar except bayrams/birthdays
 - Avoid leftovers to prevent overeating
 - Food sequencing: Eat vegetables first, then protein/fats, and carbs last
- **Hydration & Caffeine**
 - Hydration: 2–3 L/day; one glass before coffee
 - Coffee: 8:00am – 02:30pm
 - Tea: After 02:30pm
- **Movement**
 - Do 5 pull-ups before coffee
 - Post-Meal: Move or walk for 10 minutes after main meals to soak up glucose
- **Supplements**
 - Morning: Vitamin D3 + K2
 - Night: Magnesium glycinate before bed

### Day

- 06:00am Workout
- 07:30am Shake [200kcal|40g-protein];
- 12:00pm Lunch: half of yesterdays meal [700kcal|60g-protein]
- 05:00pm Cook Meal and prep Shake
- 06:00pm Dinner: half of Meal [700kcal|60g-protein]
- 06:30pm Walk
