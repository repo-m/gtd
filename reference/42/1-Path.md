## The Archetype: The Guide

The Guide I admire, ...

- Spiritual
    - lives in a being mode, not in a having mode.
    - prays, fasts and gives alms.
    - meditates.
    - lives minimally.
    - speaks less but asks the right questions.
- Physical
    - maintains a fit body and a fit spirit.
    - dresses with style and confidence.
- Mental
    - thinks independently.
    - engineers with skill in both hardware and software.
    - reflects deeply and engages intellectually.
    - writes meaningful writings.
- Social
    - listens.
    - is patient.
    - smiles.

## Callings: The Roles

- Spiritual
    - **Abd** Transcends the material by prioritizing "being" over "having" through prayer, fasting, and silence.
- Physical
    - **Steward** Maintains the body as a temple of vitality, reflecting inner discipline through aesthetic confidence and fitness.
- Mental
    - **Intellectual** Explores truth through independent reflection and writing to critique ideologies, seek Purposes, and refine Methodologies.
  - Engineer: Applies physical laws to transform complexity into elegant, reliable order through Deep Work, mastering difficult concepts rapidly to produce at the highest level of quality and speed.
- Social
    - **Anchor**
        - Husband: Nurtures deep intimacy and love.
        - Kinsmen (Father/Son/Brother): Builds a legacy of presence and reliability. Give roots & wings.
        - Friend: Secures the long-term bonds that fuel happiness.

## Milestones: The Horizon

- I will (action word/s) (object of the goal) for the purpose of (relevance/results).

### ABD

I will perform my five daily prayers with consistency and focus for the purpose of maintaining a "being mode" and spiritual grounding.

### STEWARD

I will cook a fresh, health-conscious meal daily at 5:00 PM for the purpose of establishing a nutritional routine that sustains my physical vitality and discipline.

### INTELLECTUAL

I will produce one deep-dive written reflection every month for the purpose of sharpening my ability to critique complex ideologies and clarify my own methodologies.

I will study the Feynman Lectures on Physics for four hours weekly for the purpose of revisiting first principles and cultivating a deep, intuitive understanding of the physical world.

### ENGINEER

I will apply pragmatic deep-work sessions to my current software tasks for the purpose of producing high-quality code at the speed required for modern development cycles.

### ANCHOR

I will schedule recurring, uninterrupted "Presence Blocks" with my family for the purpose of nurturing deep intimacy and providing the "roots" of reliability.

I will initiate monthly gatherings or meaningful check-ins with my close circle for the purpose of securing the long-term bonds that serve as a foundation for happiness.
