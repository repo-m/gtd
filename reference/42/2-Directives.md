# DIRECTIVES | Rules & Best Practices

## Personal Rules

- Consistency: Wake up at the same time every day to honor your internal clock.
- The Boredom Practice: Embrace boredom during Salah (5x daily) and meals to train focus and presence.
- Spiritual Nutrition: Read one Ayet with translation after each prayer.
- Digital Sanctuary: No phone use at home.
- Physical Maintenance: Adhere to the [Diet Protocol](Diet.md)
- Adopt a tool only if its positive impacts on these factors substantially outweigh its negative impacts.
- Environment over Willpower: Design your surroundings to support your goals rather than relying on self-discipline alone.

## Work Best Practices

- Output-First: Prioritize producing the work of your desired future role.
- Lean Operations: Avoid "orga" (organizational overhead) whenever possible.
- The "Always Be Quitting" Framework:
  - Knowledge: Document your knowledge and long-term plans.
  - Empowerment: Train your replacement; delegate and give power to those around you.
  - Indirection: Do not be the sole point of contact; bring others into the loop.
  - Growth: Always be learning.
