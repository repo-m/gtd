# Garden Plan

1. **Vertikutieren** Scarifying the lawn to remove moss and thatch
   - *Recommended months:* March to April
   - *Frequency:* Once a year
   - *Required tools:* Scarifier

2. **Düngen** Fertilizing the lawn to promote growth
   - *Recommended months:* April and September
   - *Frequency:* Once in spring and once in autumn
   - *Required tools:* Spreader, lawn fertilizer

3. **Mähen** Mowing the lawn to maintain the optimal height
   - *Recommended months:* April to October
   - *Frequency:* Once a week
   - *Required tools:* Lawn mower

4. **Bewässerung** Watering the lawn to ensure sufficient moisture
   - *Recommended months:* May to September
   - 20l/m2 per week
   - Check weather app on Wednesday and Sunday
   - *Frequency:*
     - Below 25°C (77°F): Generally 1-2 times a week
     - Above 25°C (77°F): Generally 2-3 times a week
   - *Required tools:* Garden hose or sprinkler

5. **Entfernung Unkraut** Removing weeds to keep the lawn healthy
   - *Recommended months:* April to October
   - *Frequency:* As needed, whenever weeds appear
   - *Required tools:* Weed digger or weed killer

6. **Belüftung** Aerating the lawn to improve soil aeration and drainage
   - *Recommended months:* September or October
   - *Frequency:* Every 1-2 years or when the soil is heavily compacted
   - *Required tools:* Garden rake or aerator
     - Gartenrechen oder Vertikutierer

7. **Kahlstellen ausbessern** Checking for bare patches and repairing with grass seeds
   - *Recommended months:* September or October
   - *Frequency:* As needed, usually in autumn or early spring
   - *Required tools:* Grass seeds, shovel, or rake
     - Grassamen, Schaufel oder Rechen
