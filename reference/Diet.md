# Diet Plan

- Nutrition & Structure
  - Daily calories: 1660 kcal
  - Daily protein: 158 g
  - Cook own meals
  - Zero sugar except bayrams/birthdays
  - Avoid leftovers to prevent overeating
  - Food sequencing: Eat vegetables first, then protein/fats, and carbs last
- Hydration & Caffeine
  - Hydration: 2–3 L/day; one glass before coffee
  - Coffee: 8:00am – 02:30pm
  - Tea: After 02:30pm
- Movement
  - Do 5 pull-ups before coffee
  - Post-Meal: Move or walk for 10 minutes after main meals to soak up glucose
- Supplements
  - Morning: Vitamin D3 + K2
  - Night: Magnesium glycinate before bed

### Day

- 07:30am Shake [180kcal|40g-protein]; Decide meal and prepare
- 12:00pm, 02:30pm, 05:00pm, 07:30pm
  - Main meal -> cook at 12:00pm
    - Monday & Thursday: Rind, Hähnchenbrust [500–600kcal|118–120g-protein]  
    - Tuesday & Friday: Fish (low-mercury) [600–700kcal|118–120g-protein]  
    - Wednesday: Veggie day  
    - Office days:  
      - Combine 12:00 + 02:30pm for canteen meal  
      - Choose fish or vegetarian  
      - Avoid heavy sauces/fried sides  
    - Weekend: Flexible  
  - Side Dish [700kcal]  
  - Sauces [200kcal]
