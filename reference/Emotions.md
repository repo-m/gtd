## Emotions

- Stress - Avoidance of important tasks, feeling overwhelmed, or lack of control.
- Anxiety - Fear of the unknown, lack of preparation, or perceived threats.
- Anger - Violation of personal boundaries, unmet expectations, or feeling powerless.
- Sadness - Loss, unfulfilled desires, or a need for connection and healing.
- Guilt - Conflict with personal values or feeling responsible for harm.
- Joy - Alignment with goals, deep connection, or experiencing something meaningful.
- Happiness - A sense of fulfillment, gratitude, or satisfaction with life circumstances.
- Frustration - Obstacles in progress or unmet expectations.
- Fear - A need for safety or readiness to address danger.
- Loneliness - A desire for connection or unmet social needs.
- Depression - Chronic disconnection from purpose, unresolved trauma, or persistent unmet emotional needs.
