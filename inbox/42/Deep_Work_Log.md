# Deep Work & Focus Log

- **Pre-Flight** If the "Definition of Ready" isn't met, don't start deep work.
- **The Shutdown Ritual:** Deep work ends when the log is updated. Once logged, the brain is free.

## Rules of Engagement

1. **Hermetic Environment:** Work in a "Vault" space with zero external incursions.
2. **Single-Tasking Only:** Zero "quick checks" of mail/comms during the block.
3. **Defined Output:** Every session must have a tangible deliverable (e.g., "Draft 3 pages").

## The Audit Method

- **Yield:** Did you produce a tangible "shippable" increment?
- **Focus:** Subjective scale (1-5) of cognitive "lock-in."
- **The Note:** Identify the "Friction" (what slowed you) or the "Flow Trigger" (what helped).
  - YYYY-MM-DD: [Yield: High/Med/Low] | [Focus: 1-5] | <Session Summary & Friction Notes>
  - example: 2026-04-02: [Yield: High] | [Focus: 5] | Finished API logic. Brown noise blocked the construction noise.
