# Brainstorming Session Agenda: Finding Your Next Startup Idea

## 1. Introduction & Intention (10 min)

- Align on purpose: “Build something people want.”
- Review YC idea filters:
  - Solves a *real* problem
  - Has a *clear target user*
  - Possible to *prototype quickly*

## 2. Problem Storm (30 min)

- Each person lists top 5 frustrating problems (personal/professional).
- Discuss:
  - Who faces this?
  - How painful is it?
  - How do they solve it now?

## 3. User-Focused Idea Mapping (30 min)

- Pick 2–3 user groups (e.g., parents, freelancers, mechanics).
- For each group:
  - What are their daily pains?
  - What would they pay for?

## 4. Quick MVP Thinking (20 min)

- Take top 3 promising ideas.
- For each:
  - What’s the *simplest* version you could build in a weekend?
  - Could you test it with 5 users?

## 5. Alignment & Next Steps (30 min)

- Vote: Which idea are we most excited about?
- Define one concrete 1-week action:
  - Prototype
  - Talk to users
  - Do deep research
