# Project portfolio

## Project Profile – 48V Bordnetzspannung (48V Vehicle Power Supply System)

- **Project Name:** 48V Bordnetzspannung – Evaluation of 48V Power Supply Concept
- **Duration:** 2025 (ongoing)
- **Employer / Customer:** Volkswagen AG
- **Role:** Department Representative and Technical Evaluator
- **Technologies / Tools:** Internal analysis and documentation tools

**Project Description:**
Reassessment of the 48V electrical architecture for future vehicle platforms. The objective was to re-evaluate earlier validation results, benchmark technology trends, and drive internal alignment on potential reintroduction of 48V systems for efficiency and power distribution optimization.

**Responsibilities:**

- Evaluated historical validation data and technical feasibility.
- Organized and led workshops with semiconductor and Tier-1 suppliers.
- Presented technical and strategic arguments for reconsidering 48V systems.
- Supported management decision-making for future architecture planning.

**Key Contributions / Results:**

- Conducted multi-stakeholder workshops with suppliers and experts.
- Prepared an updated evaluation report including new use cases and market insights.
- Convinced management to officially reintroduce 48V as a strategic topic.
- Secured inclusion of 48V systems in future vehicle architecture planning.

**Problem / Challenge:**

- 48V concept had been previously cancelled and faced internal resistance.
- Limited recent validation data and lack of ownership across departments.

**Action:**

- Reviewed historical validation results and performed a gap analysis.
- Engaged external suppliers to gather updated technological insights.
- Facilitated cross-department workshops to build consensus.
- Presented a data-driven case to management emphasizing efficiency gains.

**Result / Impact:**

- Topic officially reinstated for evaluation in future architectures.
- Renewed cross-functional alignment and supplier engagement.
- Established technical groundwork for re-assessing 48V implementation strategies.

## Project Profile – eHSB (Electronic Main Fuse Box)

- **Project Name:** eHSB – Electronic Main Fuse Box
- **Duration:** 2022–2024
- **Employer / Customer:** Volkswagen AG
- **Role:** Test Manager for ECU and Function at OEM Side
- **Technologies / Tools:** Vector CANoe, Atlassian Jira, internal OEM tools

**Project Description:**
Development of a next-generation electronic main fuse box (ECU) managing high-current power distribution (up to 800 A) within the vehicle’s electrical system. The project involved close collaboration among multiple OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers, and service partners.

**Responsibilities:**

- Established and led test management for the ECU and its software functions.
- Defined and executed test strategies across HIL, pre-series, and series vehicles.
- Managed test execution across component, integration, and system levels.
- Ensured requirement fulfillment and traceability across all test phases.

**Key Contributions / Results:**

- Built a complete test management framework from scratch.
- Created a Jira-based Kanban test process with standardized reporting templates.
- Achieved 100 % requirements coverage at component test level.
- Set up two test vehicles with dedicated test equipment.
- Planned and integrated pre-series cars with the ECU installed.
- Sourced and integrated an external automated test team and test bench.
- Framework success led to project-wide adoption; assigned change management responsibility.

**Problem / Challenge:**

1. Testing high-current ECU (up to 800 A) posed major infrastructure and safety challenges.
2. The project involved six different companies with no existing test process.
3. No test infrastructure (HIL or pre-series vehicles) was planned or available initially.

**Action:**

- Designed and implemented a test management plan and Kanban-based workflow.
- Coordinated infrastructure setup with external test partners.
- Aligned all stakeholders (OEMs, Tier-1s, service companies) under one unified process.
- Monitored Tier-1 test activities to identify and close process and traceability gaps.

**Result / Impact:**

- Established a scalable and automated test management ecosystem.
- Enabled seamless multi-partner collaboration and accelerated validation.
- Achieved complete test coverage and secured component readiness for integration.
- The main project adopted the same approach as a best-practice model.
