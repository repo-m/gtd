## Strategy ONE: Think different and more positive

1. [Thoughts detective](2022-12-03_STAGE_S1_1_Thought_Detective.md)
2. [Positive instead of negative](2022-12-03_STAGE_S1_2_Positive_Negative.md)
3. **The Inner Critic**
   - I know my Inner Critic
   - He always try to demotivate me
   - He wants me to fear the presentation
   - He is using my Lampenfieber against me
   - I will stop my Inner Critic
   - I will not listen to him before and during my presentation -> I am stronger
   - The Inner Critic is ugly and is looking dirty - he looks like ??
4. **Affirmation**
   - I can handle Lampenfieber.
   - I do not need to be perfect
   - I trust in my skills
   - If I should get nervous, I know now helpful practices and can use them immediately.
   - If my Lampenfieber will not hinder me from doing/saying what I think is important.
   - It is not a shame to be nervous.
   - Like a new sport I will master it step by step and I will control my Lampenfieber and use this important tool.
   - My Lampenfieber shows what is really matters for me - that is great (Respect, Impress other, Success, Love)
   - Without my Lampenfieber I would be boring not focused and my presentations could not be great.
5. **Thoughts Stop**
   - Inner thoughts and dialogue -> Not the Inner Critic
   - Before and during a presentation I need to focus and do not need any inner dialogue or thought. I will silence all of them and just focus.
   - I will say to them: I do not have time for you! I need to focus! Be quite! Silence now!
   - I will push the Delete button
   - I will put them in a box and lock them up

## Strategy THREE: Improve your social skills

1. **Strength-Weakness Impuls**
   1. I am aware of my skills and confident
   2. I accept my strengths on stage
      1. I am a good communicator in german and english / I have great social skills
      2. My slides are always good
      3. I am always prepared
      4. I have a brought and horizontal knowledge in topics I am presenting
   3. I accept my weaknesses on stage
      1. I have too much temperament
      2. I am nervous and excited
      3. I am not always on point -> thinking load
      4. I am not an deep expert in topics I present
2. [My Highlights](2022-12-03_STAGE_S3_2_My_Highlights.md) --> Monthly Planning

## Strategy FOUR: Score with body language and voice

1. [Posture in everyday life](2022-09-01_Posture.md)

   3. Stand/sit grounded like a tree
   4. Open arms
2. **Own tone**
