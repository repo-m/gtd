## Body language and voice

1. **Own tone**
   1. Find your own tone -> Mhhhm
   2. Practice in every day life (Phone calls, meetings, ...)
   3. Start presentation in own tone
2. **Speech breath**
   1. Abdominal breathing
   2. Shoulders down
   3. Focus on exhalation and exhale longer
3. **Anti-Stress-Breathing**
   1. Breath slow x6/Minute
   2. Focus on exhalation and exhale longer
   3. Online Meeting: Exhale with lip breaks: Fffff
   4. Affirmation:
      1. "I am breathing calm and slow"
      2. "I can always trust my breathing"
