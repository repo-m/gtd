# STAGE S2.1: Under Spotlight

1. Be calm and meditate for 1 minutes
2. Now imagine the presentation/speech situation in detail
   1. How does it look like there?
   2. Is it dark or light?
   3. How does it smell?
   4. Can I taste something?
   5. Do I have something in my hands?
   6. What do I wear? How does it feel?
3. Now it is my turn
   1. I go slow, calm, quite but safe on stage
   2. I anchor myself to the ground
   3. I look into the audience
   4. I start my speech/presentation with my strong voice in my own tone
   5. I feel the light of the presentation or spotlight
   6. I recognize the audience: the looks, the voices...
4. Whenever I feel my Lampenfieber I anchor myself to the ground at exhale
5. I am perfectly prepared and highly motivated
6. I am happy to present my ideas, thoughts ...
7. After my speech/presentation everyone is giving me a big applause, which I enjoy every bit of it
8. Than I will open my eyes.
