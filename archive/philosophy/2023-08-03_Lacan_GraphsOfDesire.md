## Graph III

![Graph III](../projects/HumanScience/2023-08-03_GraphOfDesire_Graph_III.png)

- **d**: desire
  - What is Desire? It is Need, Demand and Drive.
    - Need: Necessity w/o language => Biological needs
      - Need is always result of suffering (example: Food is the result of hunger): Life is suffering #Schopenhauer
    - Need - Necessety (through symbolisation) = Demand
    - Demand - Need = Desire
    - We desire only what the Other desires.
    - Desire to be desired by the Other. (Example A: We want the desire of our mom)
    - Desire can never be satisfied => There is always a lack (Example A: But our mom also loves our father)
      - After we get what we desire; the desire immediately shifts to a new desire
  - Object petit a: Object-cause of our Desire
    - We think that once we have the object petit a than we will fill our lack and become complete => Fantasy
      - this will never happen => Once we have the object petit a, it will change and become something else => We will always say: That is not it.
- **Che Vuoi?**: What do you want? => This leads to fantasy
- **fantasy**: Relation of Bared Subject and petit a
- The Barred Subject meets the Other A and desires what the Other desire. Therefore we first ask ourself what do the Other wants?
- The Lack is the cause of Desire
  - We fill the lack with fantasy
