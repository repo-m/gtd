## Bio

- probably studied in Milesian school by #Thales
- probably visiting Egypt which was a center of learning. 
- At the age of 40. He set up a community of around 300 people in Croton southern Italy. It's members studied a mixture of mystical and academic studies
- Pythagoras became something like a prophet for his pupils
- his community had virtually disappeared by the end of the 4th century BCE
