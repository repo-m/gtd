  * #Al-Kindi : Reason and Revelation are just two different ways of getting at the same truth
    * That would mean we don't really need revelation or religion

## Varieties of Evidentialism

* #Al-Kindi :
  * For **all** subjects and propositions: A subjects S is justified/rational in believing proposition p = S has good direct evidence of p
* #Al-Farabi :
  * For **some** subjects and propositions: A subjects S is justified/rational in believing proposition p = S has good direct evidence of p
  * The layperson may believe on the basis of testimony - the testimony of the prophet, who receives the revelation
  * Hein lies the Unique Epistemic Role of Revelation and Religion: to enable the prophet to educate the layperson

## The prophecy problem

* Faced with two competing (supposed) prophets, how can a layperson work out which to follow, without working out the relevant truth for ourselves and so making that very prophecy pointless?

## Goldman & the Problem of Expertise

* Novice/expert problem: How can we ascertain when expert claims are true, without ourselves becoming an experts?
* Novice/2expert problem: Given two conflicting expert testimonies, how do we work out which to favour, without ourselves becoming experts?
* Alvin #Goldman says
  1. Distinguish esoteric/exoteric claims - those not-understandable/understandable to layperson
  2. The layperson must somehow base decision on the expert's exoteric claims
  3. Better exoteric claims means likely better esoteric claims - so the layperson should follow the most convincing expert
* BUT: Is 3. true? Can't genuine experts be boring and unconvincing? And can't charlatans be exciting and convincing?

## Al-Farabi & the Prophet

* Goldman's problem was how to ensure that quality of rhetoric tracks quality of expertise
* For #Al-Farabi , the true prophet has not just knowledge & understanding but perfect, God-given knowledge & perfect, God-given understanding
* They don't just know & understand the truth about the world - they know & understand the layperson. Perfectly!
* And this ensures that their testimony, their prophecy, will be the most convincing.

## #Al-Farabi 's Islamic Epistemology

* The Unique Epistemic Role of Religion & Revelation is to enable the Prophet to educate the layperson to truths htey wouldn't otherwise be able to understand or discover for themselves
* The True Prophet receives perfect & complete knowledge & understanding through God-given revelation
* This ensures that their prophecy will be the most convincing
* The True Prophet will be the perfect Rhetorician
