# OKR

* sound judgment, strong leadership and a creative workplace culture => fundamentals
* the four OKR "superpowers":focus, align, track, and stretch.
* Less is more. "A few extremely well-chosen objectives impart a clear message about what we say 'yes' to and what we say 'no' to." A limit of three to five OKRS per cycle leads companies, teams, and individuals to choose what matters most. In general, each objective should be tied to five or fewer key results.
