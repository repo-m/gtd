## Brainstorm and research

- Problems and conflicts:
  - Islam vs. Science -> Whenever there is a verse which is from a sciencetific perspective is questionable the reaction can not be: "Science is wrong"
  - Argument of Ibn Rushd: Quran is one of the ayets of Allah. We are spending way too much time with the quran ayets instead also spending time to understand other ayets like nature etc.
- Main articles of faith:
  - Belief in the existence and oneness of God.
  - Belief in the existence of angels.
  - Belief in the existence of the books of which God is the author: the Quran (revealed to Muhammad), the Gospel (revealed to Jesus), the Torah (revealed to Moses), and Psalms (revealed to David).
  - Belief in the existence of prophets: Muhammad being the last of them, Jesus the penultimate, and others sent before them [like Moses, Abraham, David, Joseph, Jacob].
  - Belief in the existence of the Day of Judgment: in that day, humanity will be divided into two groups: that of paradise and that of hell. These groups are themselves composed of subgroups.
  - Belief in the existence of God's predestination (qadar, 'Divine Decree') due to God's omniscience, whether it involves good or bad.
