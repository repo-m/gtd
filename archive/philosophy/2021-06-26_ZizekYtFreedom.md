# Zizek: Freedom - possible, impossible

* 13.02.2016, Zürich
* What about the morning after? After the revolution for example in arabic spring in Egypt
  * it would be great to see the second part of the movie V wie Vendetta
* Paradox of freedom - possible vs impossible
  * everything is possible in a personal way (immortality upload your brain, technology etc.)
  * in social perspective everything is impossible. you don't have an alternative to the current system
  * Joke: It might be possible to become immortal but it is impossible to raise taxes for investing in education
