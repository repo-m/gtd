# Zizek Sufism

For Zizek, it is perverse because the subject acts not for truth or freedom, but to fulfill the imagined desire of the Other (in this case, God).
This aligns with Lacan’s notion of the pervert, who seeks to satisfy what they think the Other wants, rather than confronting the fundamental lack in the Other. So, aiming to return to divine unity becomes a submission to an illusion of divine wholeness.
