# Al Farabi, Alpharabius (872-951, Otrar)

## Thesis

* İslamın kurucu felsefecisidir 
* siyasî filozofdur
* Er war der Ansicht, dass die Philosophie nunmehr in der islamischen Welt ihre neue Heimat gefunden habe. 
* Philosophische Wahrheiten hielt er für universell gültig und betrachtete die Philosophen als Propheten, die zu ihren Erkenntnissen mittels göttlicher Inspiration (arab. waḥy) gelangt seien
* His work, aimed at synthesis of philosophy and Sufism

### Religion

* The purpose of religion is to promote true happiness
* al-Fārābī insisted with #Platon that the philosopher must be forced to return to the cave, learn to talk to its inhabitants in a manner they can comprehend, and engage in actions that may improve their lot.
