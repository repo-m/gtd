# 2023-06-04 #Zizek: Interplay of Ideology and Subconscious Actions

**The power of ideology lies not just in what we believe, but in how we act.**

Žižek's theory of ideology draws heavily on #Lacan ian psychoanalysis. He posits that ideology is not just a matter of conscious belief, but is deeply embedded in our subconscious and in our everyday actions and interactions. In other words, we 'live' our ideology, often unknowingly, through our actions, rituals, and behaviors.

One way Žižek often illustrates this is through the concept of "ideological fantasy." This is the idea that our actions are driven not just by our conscious beliefs, but also by subconscious fantasies and desires that are shaped by ideology. These ideological fantasies help to mask the inconsistencies and contradictions in our lived reality, allowing us to maintain our beliefs in the face of evidence to the contrary.

To put it differently, ideology, for Žižek, is not simply about what we think or what we believe; it's about how we behave, how we live, and how we relate to the world and others around us. This approach challenges us to consider how our actions might be unconsciously shaped by ideological forces, and calls for a more critical and self-reflective stance towards our own beliefs and actions.
