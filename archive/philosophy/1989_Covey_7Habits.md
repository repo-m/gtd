# 7 habits

## Part One: Paradigms and Principles

### Inside out

* Primary greatness: ethics, values, principles
  * more important in the long term
  * important for trust
  * Is working w/o secondary greatness
  * Costs everyday work: "You always reap what you sow; there is no shortcut"
* Secondary greatness: social skills
* Seeing and being: Being is seeing in the human dimension
* Principles: fairness, integrity, honesty, human dignity, service, quality, excellence, potential, growth, patience, nurturance, encouragement
  * principles are not practices
  * Principles are not values: Principles are territory and values are maps
  * Principles are fundamental and self-evident
* Listening involves patience, openness, and the desire to understand - highly developed qualities of character (p.37)
* 7 Habits is a principle-centered, character-based, "inside-out" approach to personal and interpersonal effectiveness (p.42)
* It says if you want to have a happy marriage, be the kind of person who generates positive energy and sidesteps negative energy rather than empowering it. If you want to have a more pleasant, cooperative teenager, be a more understanding, empathic, consistent, loving parent. If you want to have more freedom, more latitude in your job, be a more responsible, a more helpful, a more contributing employee. If you want to be trusted, be trustworthy. If you want the secondary greatness of recognized talent, focus first on primary greatness of character. (p.43)

### The 7 Habits - An Overview

* OUR CHARACTER, BASICALLY, is a composite of our habits. "Sow a thought, reap an action; sow an action, reap a habit; sow a habit reap a character; sow a character, reap a destiny," the maxim goes. (p.46)
* Habit: intersection of knowledge, skill and desire
  * Knowledge: what to do, why to do (paradigm)
  * Skill: how to do
  * Desire: want to do (motivation)
* Maturity continuum: dependence (you) -> habit 1,2,3: self-mastery -> independence (I) -> habit:4,5,6 -> interdependence (we)
* As an interdependent person, I have the opportunity to share myself deeply, meaningfully, with others, and I have access to the vast resources and potential of other human beings.
* Interdependence is a choice only independent people can make. Dependent people cannot choose to become interdependent. They don't have the character to do it; they don't own enough of them selves.
* Effectiveness: P/PC Balance
  * P: Production of desired results (golden eggs)
  * PC: Production Capability (goose)
* Three assets: physical, financial, human

# 7 Habits

## PRIVATE VICTORY

### Habit 1: BE PROACTIVE

- Pause and respond based on principles and desired results.
- Use proactive language.
- Focus on your Circle of Influence.
- Become a Transition Person.

### Habit 2: BEGIN WITH END IN MIND

- Define outcomes before you act.
- Create and live by a personal mission statement.

### Habit 3: PUT FIRST THING FIRST

- Focus on your highest priorities.
- Eliminate the unimportant.
- Plan every week.
- Stay true in the moment of choice.

## PUBLIC VICTORY

- Build your Emotional Bank Account with others.

### Habit 4: THINK WIN WIN

- Have an Abundance Mentality.
- Balance courage and consideration.
- Consider other people's wins as well as your
- Create Win-Win Agreements.

### Habit 5: SEEK FIRST TO UNDERSTAND, THEN TO BE UNDERSTOOD

- Practice Empathic Listening.
- Respectfully seek to be understood.

### Habit 6: SYNERGIZE

- Value differences.
- Seek 3rd Alternatives.

### Habit 7: SHARPEN THE SAW

- Achieve the Daily Private Victory.
- Balance production and production capability.
